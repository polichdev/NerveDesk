07 / GET CONNECTED

Simple setup.
Better flow.

Built for Windows and designed to work across your local network.

01

Get NerveDesk

Download and install NerveDesk on your Windows computers.

02

Connect devices

Bring your devices onto the same local network and discover them in the app.

03

Make space

Arrange your screens, choose the features you need, and settle into your flow.

✳ Virtual display extension requires the bundled display driver. KVM and mirroring work without it.

Good to know.

What do I need to get started?

Windows 10 or 11 and devices connected to the same local network. A wired connection or 5 GHz Wi-Fi is recommended.

Do I need a driver?

The virtual display feature requires the bundled display driver. Screen mirroring and keyboard and mouse sharing work without it.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. Mobile Remote runs in your phone's browser and uses a PIN to connect. Your phone can also be used as a camera or for Quick Share.
